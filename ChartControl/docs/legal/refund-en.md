# Refund Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-18. This policy explains refunds for purchases made on {{BRAND_NAME}}. It is not legal advice; where local consumer law grants you stronger rights, those rights prevail._

## 1. What you buy here

{{BRAND_NAME}} is software. What you can buy here is **use of that software** — points (spent on in-app features such as AI analysis runs and saving your own indicators) and any paid plan offered in the app. You are not buying a financial product, an investment, a trading service, or a promise about market outcomes. **We do not hold your trading funds** — deposits, withdrawals and trades happen at your connected exchange, not here. This policy covers only purchases made on {{BRAND_NAME}}.

**What a paid feature delivers.** When you spend points on an AI analysis run, what you have bought
and received is **the analysis itself** — the run completed and its output was shown to you. You are
not buying a correct answer, a profitable answer, or a particular directional view. An analysis that
turned out to disagree with what the market later did was still delivered.

## 2. Unused points — 7-day refund

If you purchased points and have **not used them**, you may request a full refund within **7 days** of purchase. This reflects the cooling-off right for digital content under applicable consumer law (e.g. Korea's Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce).

- Refunds are issued to the **original payment method**.
- **Once you have used any of the points, the purchase is no longer refundable**, because the digital service has begun. Please decide before you spend your points.

## 3. Used / consumed points

Points that have already been **spent** (e.g. an AI analysis run, a saved item, an extension) are **non-refundable**, because the digital service was already delivered.

## 4. Subscriptions (if applicable)

If a recurring plan is offered, you may cancel at any time; cancellation stops the next renewal. Time already elapsed in the current period is non-refundable unless local law requires otherwise.

## 5. What is NOT refundable

- **Trading losses.** We cannot refund market losses. This is because of how the Service works, not
  merely because we say so:
  - **Every order is entered and approved by you.** We never create, size, modify or cancel an order
    on our own initiative, and no output of the Service reaches your exchange until you approve an
    order yourself (Terms §2-3, §2-4).
  - **Your trades happen at your own exchange, not here.** Price, execution, fees and settlement are
    the exchange's, and your funds never leave your exchange account into ours (Terms §1, §2-1).
  - **The Service's output is reference material, not advice.** Indicators, backtests, signal rules
    and AI output — including any directional view the AI states — describe a chart. They are not
    personalised advice, not a recommendation, and not a solicitation, and they are not tailored to
    your capital, leverage or risk tolerance (Terms §2-5, §2A).
  - **A signal rule is one you wrote.** You chose the condition, instrument and timeframe. We do not
    review it for soundness (Terms §4-2).
  - **We promise no result.** No profit is guaranteed and no accuracy rate is claimed, promised or
    implied (Terms §2-7, §2A-4).
  - A loss therefore arises from market movement and from your own decision, neither of which we
    control or can reverse.
- **A directional view or analysis you disagreed with, or that the market went against.** The paid
  feature delivered is the analysis run, not a correct forecast (see §1).
- **Exchange fees / funding** charged by your exchange (KuCoin, etc.) — those are governed by the exchange.
- Points received through promotions, referrals or gifts (they were not purchased).

**Where we do refund.** If a paid feature did not actually deliver — an analysis run that failed,
was cut off, or returned nothing, or points deducted twice for one run — that is our fault and we
refund the points. Tell us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and you do not need to argue the case; we can see
the run in our records.

Nothing in this section limits rights you have under consumer law that cannot be excluded by
agreement.

## 6. Payment-provider chargebacks

Purchases are processed by third-party payment providers. You may also use the provider's own dispute process, but contacting us first is usually faster.

## 7. How to request a refund

Email **{{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}** with your account email and the approximate purchase date. We aim to reply within 1 business day. **Once a refund is approved, we return it within 3 business days** to the original payment method. The time it then takes to appear on your statement depends on your payment provider, not on us.
