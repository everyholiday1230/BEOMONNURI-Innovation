# Risk Disclosure

Effective: 18 September 2026 · Version 1.1

## Read this first

**Trading cryptocurrency perpetual futures can lose you more than you put in.**

This is not boilerplate. Each risk below happens in practice, and while you use this
Service you bear it yourself.

## 1. Leverage

- Leverage multiplies losses by the same factor as gains. At 10× leverage, a 10%
  move against you **wipes out your margin.**
- A little further and you are liquidated. Liquidation does not wait for your
  confirmation.

## 2. Liquidation

- When margin runs short, **the exchange closes your position by force.** The
  Company cannot prevent it and it cannot be undone.
- In fast markets or thin liquidity, the fill can be worse than the liquidation
  price, and **losses can exceed your margin.**
- The Company attempts to alert you when liquidation risk crosses a threshold, but
  **delivery of that alert is not guaranteed.** It may be late or absent if:
  - the exchange does not publish a liquidation price
  - there is a server or network failure
  - you have turned notifications off
  - the price moves within seconds

**Do not leave a position unattended relying on alerts.**

## 3. Funding

Perpetual futures pay or charge funding at intervals. You can be right about
direction and still lose money to accumulated funding.

## 4. Market risk

- Cryptocurrency prices move sharply in short periods. Double-digit percentage moves
  in a day are not unusual.
- The market is open 24 hours. **Your position moves while you sleep.**
- In thin markets you may not be able to exit at the price you want.

## 5. Technical risk

- **If the exchange is down, your order is not transmitted.** You must then manage
  positions at the exchange directly. Our screen may load while orders cannot go out.
- Market data can lag. The price on screen may differ from the exchange's price, and
  your order is processed **at the price the exchange receives.**
- Network problems can leave you unable to confirm whether an order was accepted. In
  that case **check at the exchange.** Re-sending the same order while the outcome is
  unknown can double your position.

## 6. About analysis, signals and the AI's view

- Indicators, backtests, signal rules and AI output are **reference information.** They
  are not personalised investment advice and are not a recommendation or solicitation to
  make any particular trade.
- **The AI may state a directional view.** When it does, that is the software's reading of
  chart data, offered as reference material for you to weigh. It is not an instruction to
  trade, it is not tailored to your capital or risk tolerance, and **it can be wrong.** No
  accuracy rate is claimed, promised or implied. If you place an order after reading it,
  that order is your decision and your risk.
- **A signal rule is one you wrote.** You define the condition; the Service evaluates it
  against the candle series and marks where it occurred. A mark states that a condition
  happened — it does not state that acting on it would be profitable. We do not review
  your rule for soundness.
- **A detection is not a forecast.** "The MACD line crossed above its signal line at 09:15"
  is a description of what the data did. It says nothing about what happens next.
- **Past performance does not indicate future results.** A backtest is a calculation
  over historical data; real trading differs because of execution delay, slippage,
  fees and funding.
- Following a strategy is a **bookmark.** It does not copy signals or submit orders.
  Every order is reviewed and executed by you.

## 7. Limits that come from how we are built

- **The Company does not hold your funds.** They are in your exchange account, so if
  something happens to the exchange the Company cannot return them on its behalf.
- **The Company does not process deposits or withdrawals.** Do those at the exchange.
- The Company cannot change your exchange account state (tier, fee rate, limits).

## 8. Taxes

Reporting tax on your trading results is your responsibility. The Company does not
file on your behalf and does not provide tax advice.

## 9. Regulation

Cryptocurrency derivatives trading is prohibited or restricted in some countries. It
is your responsibility to check the law where you live.

## 10. Only what you can afford to lose

**Trade only with money whose loss would not affect your life.**

Do not trade derivatives with borrowed money, living expenses, or funds you need
soon. In leveraged trading, losses grow faster than expected.
