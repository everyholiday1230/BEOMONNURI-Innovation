### 1.5 Trading records kept for improving the Service

We keep a separate record of **what you had on screen when you placed an order and how it turned out** — the indicators you had enabled, the market conditions at that moment, the risk checks that ran, and the result.

| Data | Purpose | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Indicators enabled, market snapshot, risk-check outcome, order result | Improving the Service, including **training our AI models** | 5 years |

**How this is kept**

- It is stored under a **separate pseudonymous key**, not your account identifier.
- **If you close your account, the pseudonymous record remains.** It can no longer be linked back to you by us, and it is not deleted when your account is.
- It is never shared with or sold to anyone outside our company.

**If you do not want this**

Tell us at the support address in §11 and we will stop adding new records for you. Records already collected under the pseudonymous key cannot be located and removed, because they are no longer linked to your account — this is the consequence of pseudonymising them.

> **This section describes AI model training on your trading records.** If that is not something you want, do not connect an exchange account, or contact us before trading.

## 2. What we do not collect

- **No identity documents, passports or face photos.** We do not hold your funds, so we bear no identity-verification duty; collecting identity data without a legal basis to keep it would only create exposure risk for you. Identity verification is performed by the exchange.
- **No national identification numbers.**
- **No bank account or card details.** We do not handle deposits or withdrawals.
- **No withdrawal permission on your exchange account is requested.**

## 3. Legal bases

- **Performance of a contract**: account and exchange connection data are needed to provide the Service.
- **Legitimate interests**: access records are needed to block unauthorised access and protect accounts.
- **Legal obligation**: trading and consent records are kept for dispute handling and statutory compliance.
- **Consent**: processing beyond these purposes (for example marketing email) is done only with your separate consent, and you can use the Service without giving it.

For residents of the European Economic Area, GDPR Article 6(1)(b), (f), (c) and (a) apply respectively.

## 8. Security measures

- Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes; the original is never kept.
- Exchange API keys are stored encrypted.
- Transport is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Administrative reads and changes are recorded with the operator and time (audit log).
- Administrative permissions are split by duty. For example, support staff can view user information but cannot change account status.
- Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed periodically.

**No system is perfectly secure.** We take reasonable measures, but it matters that you do not reuse your password on other services and do not grant withdrawal permission to your exchange API keys.

## 9. Cookies

We use only a session cookie to keep you signed in and a security cookie to prevent forged requests. **We use no advertising, tracking or behavioural cookies, and we embed no third-party advertising scripts.**

## 10. Children

The Service may not be used by anyone under 19, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Accounts found to belong to someone under that age are deleted without delay.
