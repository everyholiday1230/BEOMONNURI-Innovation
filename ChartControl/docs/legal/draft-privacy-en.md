## 2. What we do not collect

- **No identity documents, passports or selfies.** The Company does not hold funds,
  so it has no identity-verification obligation. Collecting identity data without a
  legal basis to keep it would create exposure risk for you and no benefit.
- **No national identification or bank account numbers.**
- **No withdrawal permission on your exchange account is requested.**

## 3. Why we need it (legal basis)

- Performance of contract: account and exchange connection data are needed to
  provide the Service.
- Legitimate interests: access records are needed to block unauthorised access.
- Legal obligation: some records are kept for dispute handling and accounting.

## 8. How we protect it

- Passwords are stored hashed only; plain text is never kept.
- Exchange API keys are stored encrypted.
- Transport is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Administrative reads and changes are recorded with the operator and time.
- Administrative permissions are split by duty — for example, support staff can view
  user information but cannot change account status.

**No system is perfectly secure.** We take reasonable measures, but it matters that
you do not reuse your password elsewhere and do not grant withdrawal permission to
your exchange keys.
